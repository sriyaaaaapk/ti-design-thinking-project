## Scenario Analysis : 

note: Ambient measure the environment and sun. Reflection of particular panel area.

**Case 1 Sunny Day**

Ambient = High; Power = High; Reflection = High
Interpretation: Panel healthy no cleaning

**Case 2 Cloudy Day**
Ambient = Low; Power = Low; Reflection = High
Interpretation: Expected behavior as sun is low. no cleaning.

**Case 3 Dusty Panel**
Ambient = High; Power = Low; Reflection = Low
Interpretation: Possible contamination. Cleaning required

**Case 4 Dead Cell / Electrical Fault**
Ambient = High; Power = Low; Reflection = High
Interpretation: Panel surface looks clean but power is low, so not contamination could be possible electrical issue.

**Case 5 above continuation hotspot**
Ambient = High; Power = Low; Reflection = High; Temperature = High
Interpretation: Possible cell degradation Possible hotspot Maintenance required no cleaning!
